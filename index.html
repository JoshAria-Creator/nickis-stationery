Questions people ask

How long will my order take?

It depends on where your items come from. Items found in Port Moresby arrive faster than items sourced from elsewhere in PNG or overseas. We tell you the expected delivery time with your quote, before you pay.

How fast will I get a quote, and how long is it valid?

We usually send your quote the same day. Quotes are valid for 7 days, because supplier prices can change.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order a single item or a full school supply list.

Do I pay everything up front?

For smaller orders, yes: full payment before we source. For larger and overseas orders, we ask for a 50% deposit and you pay the balance on delivery.

What if you cannot find an item?

We offer you a similar alternative, or we source it internationally. Nothing is added to your order until you approve it.

Can schools and departments get paperwork?

Yes. We provide written quotes, invoices and receipts.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere within Port Moresby city for K50. Outside the city for K100, including villages along the Magi Highway, such as Barakau and Tubusereia, and the Hiritano and Koiari areas. Delivery is free on orders over K10,000.

Can you source from overseas?

Yes. We source locally, nationally and internationally, so hard-to-find items are not a problem. Overseas orders take longer, and we confirm the timing with your quote.
